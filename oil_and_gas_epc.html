Overview

Complete EPC Solutions for Oil & Gas

EPC stands for Engineering, Procurement and Construction. EPC companies are hired by Oil & Gas companies for long-term construction projects.

Marwar Projects is an Oil and Gas EPC contractor providing services across the oil, gas and power industry. Our services are widely demanded in the market for their speed and quality, and clients value the quality and quantity of our work at a reasonable cost.

What EPC Means

Engineering, Procurement & Construction

One contractor, one point of responsibility, from design to commissioning.

E

Engineering

Design and planning of the plant, piping and systems.

P

Procurement

Sourcing of materials and equipment on time and at fair cost.

C

Construction

Site execution and delivery with quality and safety.
